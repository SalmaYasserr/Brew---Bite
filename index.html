More Than Just a Café

Brew & Bite began with a simple idea: a neighborhood spot where good coffee and honest food bring people together. We bake in the morning, brew to order and cook with ingredients we are proud to serve.

Soft light, warm wood and green corners make it easy to stay a while, whether you are catching up with friends or working with a flat white.
